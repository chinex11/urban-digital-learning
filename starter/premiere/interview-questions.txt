URBAN DIGITAL LEARNING · INTERVIEW QUESTION BANK (Premiere Pro)
Ask open questions; let silence work; ask the subject to include the question in the answer
("I started casting bronze when...") so the quote stands alone without your voice.

WARM-UP (not for the edit)
  - Tell me your name and what you do here, the way you'd introduce yourself to a stranger.
STORY
  - How did you first get into this work? Tell me about the first day.
  - Who taught you? What's one thing they told you that you still remember?
  - Tell me about a time when this work was really hard.
  - Walk me through what you're doing with your hands right now.
MEANING
  - What would be lost if nobody did this work any more?
  - What do you want young people to know about it?
  - When you finish a piece, how do you feel?
CLOSE
  - Is there anything I didn't ask that you want to say?
TECH CHECKLIST: lav or phone mic close to the mouth · 10 s of room tone · release form signed
