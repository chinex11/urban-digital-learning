URBAN DIGITAL LEARNING · CAPSTONE CLIENT BRIEF
Client: Edo Fresh Market — Rebrand

BACKGROUND
Family-owned grocery on Airport Road, Benin City, open 12 years. Known for fresh produce,
fair prices and friendly staff. Current logo: red text in a yellow oval, made 12 years ago.
Customers call it "Edo Fresh". The owner's son is launching WhatsApp delivery.

PROBLEM
Younger shoppers (20-35) see the brand as old and "for our parents". New supermarkets
with modern branding are opening nearby.

GOALS
- Feel fresh, modern and trustworthy to 20-35 year olds
- Keep loyal older customers (don't lose the name or all recognition)
- Support the new WhatsApp delivery service

MUST STAY
- The name "Edo Fresh Market"
- Some link to the old brand (owner loves the yellow)

DELIVERABLES
1. Logo system: primary, icon, one-colour and reversed versions
2. Palette (with HEX/RGB/CMYK) and type system
3. Shopfront sign mockup (3 m x 1 m) — large format rules apply
4. Delivery bag/sticker design (vinyl-cut friendly version)
5. WhatsApp/Instagram post 1080 x 1350 announcing delivery
6. One-page brand sheet + organised file pack

PROCESS
v1: 2 concepts → client picks direction + round 1 feedback → v2 → round 2 feedback → final + sign-off

TIMELINE: 2 weeks. BUDGET: Mid-size local business.
