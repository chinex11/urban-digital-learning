Project: Brand mini-kit — Mama Ngozi's Kitchen

ABOUT: Home-style Nigerian food delivery in Benin City, run by Mama Ngozi and her daughter.
  Popular dishes: jollof rice, pepper soup, egusi with pounded yam, small chops for events.
  Orders come through WhatsApp and Instagram; delivery by motorbike across Benin City.
AUDIENCE: Busy workers and students (22-40) who miss home cooking; event planners for small chops.
WHAT MAKES THEM DIFFERENT: Real family recipes, generous portions, always on time.
THE CLIENT SAYS: "I want people to feel like they are eating in their mother's house,
  but I don't want it to look old-fashioned or like a roadside buka."
COMPETITORS: Chicken Republic, Kilimanjaro, many small Instagram food vendors.
DELIVERABLES:
  1. Logo (combination mark) + icon-only version
  2. Colour palette (3-5 colours with roles) and type system
  3. Business card 90 x 55 mm
  4. Instagram post 1080 x 1350 px announcing "Free delivery this week"
  5. One-page brand sheet + logo pack (colour, black, white; PNG + vector)
BUDGET: Small business — keep it simple and practical
