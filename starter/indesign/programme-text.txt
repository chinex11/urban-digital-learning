WELCOME
Dear friends, welcome to the Edo/Delta Youth Summit 2026. This year's theme, "Rise & Build", calls every young person to grow in faith, build useful skills and lead with integrity. Over the next few hours you will hear from leaders, learn practical skills in workshops and meet people who will become friends and partners. Take notes, ask questions and connect with someone new. We are proud of you and excited for what you will build.
— The Organising Committee
