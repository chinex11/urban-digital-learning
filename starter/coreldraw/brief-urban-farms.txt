URBAN DIGITAL LEARNING · STARTER BRIEF
Project: Logo — Urban Farms

ABOUT: Agro business near Benin City. Grows vegetables and plantain; supplies restaurants,
  hotels and supermarkets; sells fresh produce boxes to families via WhatsApp.
AUDIENCE: Restaurant owners and supermarket buyers (B2B); busy families (B2C).
VALUES: Fresh, reliable, modern farming, local pride.
FEEL: Fresh, trusted, modern — not rustic or old-fashioned.
MUST WORK ON: Delivery van (vinyl), produce box labels (1-colour print), Instagram profile,
  staff T-shirts (embroidery or screen print), invoices.
DELIVERABLES: Icon + wordmark; horizontal and stacked lockups; colour, black and white
  versions; logo pack (CDR master, PDF, SVG, transparent PNG 500 px + 2000 px).
AVOID: Clip-art tractors, overly detailed leaves, gradients in the core logo.
